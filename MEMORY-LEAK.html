About MEMORY//LEAK
You are a cybersecurity analyst defending NEXUS from evolving cyber threats. Every incident must be detected, investigated and contained before system integrity is lost.
ATTACK→ALERT→INVESTIGATE→DEFEND→CONSEQUENCE→RECOVER→ZERO ADAPTS
You are not simply answering cybersecurity questions. You physically operate inside a 3D SOC environment — investigating incidents, choosing evidence to inspect, selecting a defensive response, and dealing with the consequences of your decisions.
NEXUS
NEXUS is the fictional organization you are protecting. Its users, accounts, endpoints, network infrastructure and sensitive data are under continuous cyber attack.
PLAYER
You are the cybersecurity/SOC analyst responsible for detecting, investigating and containing incidents inside NEXUS.
  • Receives alerts
  • Travels through the 3D SOC
  • Investigates evidence
  • Identifies the threat
  • Chooses a defensive action
  • Tries to protect system integrity
ZERO
ZERO is the adaptive attacker behind the incidents. It watches how you defend NEXUS — the types of threats you encounter, what evidence you investigate, which defenses you choose, how completely you respond, and the patterns in your decisions.
Your behaviour influences future attacks. ZERO is not a traditional fixed boss — it adapts its future attack strategy based on your previous behaviour.

Threat Database
Know the threat. Investigate the evidence. Choose the right defense.
PHISHING+

An attacker sends a fake email or message designed to trick someone into clicking a malicious link, opening an attachment, or revealing sensitive information.

Investigate the sender, URL and message context before deciding how to respond.

SOCIAL ENGINEERING+

An attacker manipulates a person into trusting them or revealing information by pretending to be someone they know, such as a colleague, manager or trusted organization.

Verify the person's identity and do not trust a request simply because it appears urgent or authoritative.

CREDENTIAL THEFT+

An attacker attempts to steal usernames, passwords or other login information so they can access an account.

Investigate the suspicious login and protect the account using actions such as MFA, password reset or access revocation.

MALWARE+

Malicious software that is designed to damage systems, steal information, spy on users, or give attackers unauthorized access.

Investigate the affected endpoint and contain the threat before it spreads.

NETWORK INTRUSION+

An attacker gains unauthorized access to a network or attempts to move through network systems that they should not control.

Analyze suspicious network activity and use monitoring, firewall or blocking controls to contain the intrusion.

DATA EXFILTRATION+

An attacker secretly transfers sensitive information out of an organization without authorization.

Identify suspicious data movement, protect sensitive resources and contain unauthorized access.

RANSOMWARE+

Malicious software that locks, encrypts or disrupts systems and data, allowing attackers to demand something in return.

Protect critical systems, isolate the affected area and recover systems before the damage spreads.

Mission Outcomes
SECURED
The threat was correctly identified and contained with minimal impact.
CONTAINED
The threat was stopped, but the response caused some damage or loss of integrity.
BREACHED
The response failed to stop the attack and system integrity suffered significantly.
System integrity starts at 100%. If it reaches 0%, NEXUS is breached and the run ends.
